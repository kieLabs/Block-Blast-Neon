🎯 Goal

Move falling neon blocks, complete horizontal lines, clear them from the board and earn the highest score. The game gets faster as your level rises.

📱 Touch controls

  • Tap the board: rotate the current block.
  • Swipe left / right: move the block in that direction. Longer swipes move it farther.
  • Swipe down: soft-drop the block; a quick long downward swipe performs a hard drop.
  • Buttons below the board: LEFT, ROTATE, RIGHT, HOLD DOWN, HARD DROP and PAUSE.

⌨️ Keyboard controls

  • ← / → Move left or right.
  • ↑ or X Rotate clockwise; Z rotates backwards.
  • ↓ Soft drop; hold it to move down faster.
  • SPACE Hard drop straight down.
  • P Pause or resume; ESC exits fullscreen or closes this panel.

⭐ Score, levels and speed

  • Clearing lines awards points; clearing multiple lines at once earns more.
  • Every 10 cleared lines advances a level.
  • Each new level increases automatic falling speed. Soft drop and hard drop help you control the pace.
  • Your best score is saved on this device.

🧩 Next blocks and ghost piece

The NEXT BLOCKS previews show upcoming pieces. The translucent outline on the board shows where the active block will land if dropped.

⚙ Settings

Use Settings to turn sound effects, background music and vibration on or off, and adjust volume. Audio may begin only after your first tap because of browser restrictions.
